## IDM boundary

The reconciled model treats IDM as a structural reference and lifecycle concept. A valid IDM takeout confirms the relevant structural swing point.

Major/minor inducement distinctions remain visible as source-specific concepts and must not be collapsed merely because the same term is used.

## Engineering Liquidity

The later reconciliation canonicalized Engineering Liquidity as a core-liquidity reference derived from the most recent valid pullback immediately preceding the active Extreme OF or Extreme OB.

An invalid pullback or arbitrary pivot cannot create ENG_LQD.

A sweep of Engineering Liquidity does not itself create reversal, BOS or CHoCH.
