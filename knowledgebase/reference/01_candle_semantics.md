## Source-backed findings already reconciled

### Candle extremes

The reconciliation source map records that TrueSMC123 explicitly accepts a break of the previous candle extreme by wick or candle close in valid-pullback qualification. Candle color does not change that acceptance.

### Candle extreme protection

The 21-Day Bootcamp and TrueSMC 2026 describe bullish and bearish candle-level sequences where one extreme is broken while the opposite extreme remains protected.

### Equal extremes

TrueSMC123 and the 21-Day Bootcamp contain the equal-extreme reference-transfer case: when consecutive relevant highs or lows are equal, the second candle becomes the active reference rather than the first.

### Internal candle sequence

TrueSMC 2026 describes:
- bullish: Open → Low → High → Close
- bearish: Open → High → Low → Close

### Boundary

Inside Bar and Outside Bar observations remain distinct from structural events. The current source set does not establish a complete deterministic Outside-Bar intrabar contract. Reversal formations are likewise not automatically structural confirmation.
