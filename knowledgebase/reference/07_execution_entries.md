## Source/reconciliation notes

The source corpus contains both same-timeframe and HTF-to-LTF execution paths.

The canonical reconciliation separates:
1. methodology entry authorization;
2. order submission;
3. broker fill;
4. open-position state.

For direct candle confirmation, the completed confirmation-candle close is the methodology reference price.

Sweep or mitigation alone is insufficient to authorize entry.

Broker order type remains a platform-order policy rather than an inferred methodology fact.

Qualitative source examples remain examples unless the reconciliation explicitly formalizes them.
