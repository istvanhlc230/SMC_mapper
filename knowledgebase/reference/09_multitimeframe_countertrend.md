## HTF to LTF

The source material describes a top-down route where HTF POI or core-liquidity interaction can shift execution to LTF. The relevant LTF reference is not an arbitrary pivot; the reconciled route uses the most recently formed relevant LTF valid pullback or inducement reference.

## Countertrend

Countertrend material is kept separate because it contains scenario-specific execution and target logic rather than replacing the standard pro-trend structural lifecycle.

Read the two countertrend supplementary sources together when a countertrend question is asked. Do not promote a countertrend example into a universal structural rule without explicit reconciliation.
