## Reconciled structural ordering

1. A relevant IDM takeout confirms the structural swing point.
2. Retracement depth and candle-structure qualification are evaluated after swing confirmation.
3. That qualification determines whether a later external break can qualify as VALID_BOS.
4. Insufficient retracement does not erase the historical swing confirmation; it prevents the attempted continuation break from qualifying as BOS and shifts the active pullback/IDM reference for the next attempt.

The former SWING_CANDIDATE ordering is not canonical.
